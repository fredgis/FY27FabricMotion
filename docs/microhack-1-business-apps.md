# Micro Hack 1 — Business Apps with Fabric & Rayfin

**Business Apps on Microsoft Fabric · One-day hands-on format**
*Morning: training & demos · Afternoon: hack · 09:00 – 17:30*

> ### Scenario · Helios Mobility *(fictional client)*
>
> Helios Mobility runs a shared e-scooter fleet across several European cities.
> Ride, battery and maintenance data already land in Microsoft Fabric (OneLake),
> but city operations managers still chase problems across spreadsheets and email
> threads.
>
> **Your mission:** write a short spec and use Rayfin to generate a **Fleet
> Operations app** on Fabric. Operations managers should see every scooter's live
> status, spot the units that need attention, and assign maintenance jobs to field
> technicians — all in one place.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Audience** | Business and IT teams at the end customer — the people who will own and use the app (operations, line-of-business, data). |
| **Approach** | Spec-driven — describe the app in plain language, generate it on Fabric with Rayfin. |
| **You will learn** | How Fabric and OneLake provide the data foundation for apps · The Rayfin spec-driven app lifecycle · How to extend a generated app with workflows and roles. |
| **Prerequisites** | Access to a Microsoft Fabric workspace with capacity · A laptop with a modern browser and corporate sign-in · Basic familiarity with your own business processes and data. |
| **You will leave with** | A working Fleet Operations app, demoed at the close · A reusable spec you can adapt to your own scenarios. |

## Agenda

*One day, 09:00 – 17:30. The morning builds shared understanding through training
and live demos; the afternoon is a hands-on hack where teams build and demo.*

| Time | Session | What happens |
| ---- | ------- | ------------ |

| 10:15 – 10:30 | Break | — |

| 12:15 – 13:00 | Lunch | — |

### Afternoon — Hack

| Time | Session | What happens |
| ---- | ------- | ------------ |
| 13:00 – 13:30 | Kick-off & setup | Teams pick a city dataset, confirm workspace access, run an environment health check. |
| 13:30 – 15:30 | Hack sprint 1 — Build | Write the spec and generate the Fleet Operations app: list scooters, show live status, filter by city. |
| 15:30 – 15:45 | Break | — |

## Logistics & success criteria

**Roles**
- Lead facilitator — runs the agenda, keeps time, frames the scenario.
- Technical coaches — 1 per 2 – 3 teams, unblock during the hack.
- Data specialist — on hand for Fabric workspace and data questions.

**What participants should bring**
- A laptop that can sign in with their corporate identity.
- Curiosity about the business process they want to turn into an app.

**Prepare before the day**
- Fabric workspace(s) with capacity assigned and access granted.
- Sample datasets and a starter spec for the scenario.

**What "good" looks like at 17:30**
- Every team demos a working result, however small.
- Each team can describe how it would roll the app out to real users.
